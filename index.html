💡 Key Insights, Risk Assessment & Budget Analysis
🎯 Dominant Cost Driver Diesel consumption is the single largest expense item at ₹28,048L (28.4%) of total OPEX. This indicates high operational dependence on equipment usage and fuel prices, making diesel cost management critical for budget control.
📈 Concentration Risk Analysis The top 10 cost items represent 94.8% of the budget, showing significant concentration in specific expense areas. This requires careful monitoring and control of these high-impact items to avoid cost overruns.
💰 Statutory Impact on Total Cost Taxes and statutory payments (₹42,588L) add 43.1% to the operational budget, increasing total project cost to ₹139,779L. Royalty alone accounts for 73.5% of statutory payments.
📊 Category Distribution Pattern Operations + Repair & Maintenance together comprise 85.8% of OPEX (₹84,765L). This two-category concentration reflects the capital-intensive nature of mining operations.
🔧 Maintenance Cost Breakdown Within Repair & Maintenance (₹41,230L), the largest components are: Spares ₹17,940L (43.5%), Workshop Services ₹14,570L (35.3%), and Tyres ₹5,584L (13.5%), indicating heavy equipment depreciation.
⛏️ Production Economics Coal production plan of 2.25 MT with ₹98,728L OPEX translates to approximately ₹4,387 per MT (before statutory costs). Adding T&D makes it ₹6,212 per MT, critical for price competitiveness.
⚡ Critical Monitoring Areas Priority items for monthly tracking: (1) Diesel consumption & pricing, (2) Spares availability & costs, (3) Equipment maintenance schedules, (4) Manpower productivity, (5) Regulatory compliance for statutory payments.
📋 Budget Variance Risk With 94.8% of budget concentrated in top 10 items, a 5% variance in any of these could impact total budget by up to ₹4,678L. Contingency planning and active cost control mechanisms are essential.
✅ Recoverables & Offsets Total recoverables amount to ₹1,536L, which partially offset statutory costs, reducing effective T&D burden. However, recovery depends on regulatory changes (electricity duty, R&R in change-in-law scenarios).
